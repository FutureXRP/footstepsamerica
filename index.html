The Conductor

Harriet Tubman is about five feet tall, and she is the most wanted woman in Maryland.

When Harriet crosses into Pennsylvania, she is free. She later says she looked at her hands to see if she was the same person. And then she does the thing that makes her a legend.

She goes back.

Connections · Courage for others Harriet was already free. Every trip back was a choice to risk everything she had gained for someone else. Ask together: who would you go back for?
